FAQs

How does URL shortening work?

URL shortening works by taking a long URL and converting it into a shorter, more manageable link that redirects to the original address when clicked.

Do I need to create an account to use the URL shortening service?

You can shorten links without an account, but signing up unlocks analytics, custom slugs and link management.

Are the shortened URLs permanent? Will they expire?

Links on paid plans never expire. Links on the free plan stay active as long as they are in use.

Are there any limitations on the number of URLs I can shorten?

The free plan has generous limits. Paid plans raise or remove them entirely.

Can I customize the shortened URLs to reflect my brand or content?

Yes. Choose your own slug and, on paid plans, your own branded domain.

Can I track the performance of my shortened URLs?

Yes. Every link comes with click, location and device analytics.

How secure is the URL shortening service? Are my shortened links protected against spam or malicious activity?

Links are scanned for spam and malware, and abusive links are disabled.

What is an API key and what can I do with it?

An API key lets your applications create and manage links programmatically.

Is there an API available for integrating the URL shortening service into my applications or website?

Yes. The REST API is available on the Teams plan.
